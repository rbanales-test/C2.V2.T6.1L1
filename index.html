Protocol Active: MIDI 1.0 (Sequential Architecture)

Bank Select does nothing on its own. It saves a number, used when the next Program Change arrives. If you send the Program Change alone, the synth uses whatever bank it used last (the Latched Bank Error). Controllers are limited to 7 bits (128 steps), causing stair-step jumps.

Analogy: a postal address. Bank Select is the street. Program Change is the house number. If you send only the house number, the letter goes to whichever street you used last.
Core concept: MIDI 1.0 is blind and sequential; it agrees on numbers, but not on what they mean. MIDI 2.0 fixes the architecture: messages are unified (no latched state errors), resolution is continuous (up to 32 bits), and communication is bidirectional (devices tell the DAW their patch names).
